Official Testimonies: The Villagers & The Boys
Dr. Ionescu: "I left the tavern about 10:30, going straight to my house. Shortly before eleven, young Tristan came for me. His mother was giving birth. I was at their cottage until sometime after midnight, when the mayor came to fetch me."
Gregor Pularis: "When the rains started, I ran for home. My wife was already asleep. I put on a kettle for tea and had a cup. I was just changing out of my wet clothes when the boys knocked on my door."
Marie Pularis: "My cottage is far from the tavern. Halfway there, I changed my mind and ducked into the church to wait out the rain. My husband says I came in a few minutes after midnight."
Eric Havardi: "I ran from the tavern to my own cottage. It is down the road from where my cousin was staying. I could see his window; the light was flickering in his fireplace. I heard nothing until the wolves started howling."
The Two Boys (Matthias & Companion): "We checked the mud outside. No human footprints. Just wolf prints. The American was on the floor. Blood was glistening in the wound. It had to be fresh, no more than fifteen minutes."
Medical Report & Cause of Death
Autopsy Summary: Cause of death was a cut jugular vein by a jagged knife or claws. Radial arteries, femoral veins, and subclavian arteries were all severed after death.
The Conflict: The militia report says the body was in the water for at least six hours. But the boys saw liquid, glistening blood at the cottage at midnight.
