The Carpathian Shadow 🕯️

Embark on a dark RPG adventure set in a secluded Transylvanian village. After your friend Tim discovers a cryptic family diary, you travel deep into the mountains only to find yourselves trapped in a town gripped by folklore and fear.

Interact with mysterious NPCs to peel back layers of deception, collect hidden clues to fill your evidence log, and solve the ultimate mystery of Werner’s legacy before the shadows close in. Every choice determines if you escape or become part of the village's grim history.
