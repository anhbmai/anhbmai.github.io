A trip to the forgotten village in the Carpathian Mountains of Transylvania..

After discovering his grandfather’s abandoned diary written in Romanian which has clues leading to a forgotten village deep in the Carpathian Mountains of Transylvania, your friend Tim Johnson convinces you to travel there in search of the truth behind his grandfather’s mysterious death decades ago. But upon arriving, you quickly realize the village was hidden for a reason - strange howls echo through the forest, a mysterious death, and something inhuman stalks the streets after dark. As the secrets buried within the diary begin to unfold, one horrifying truth becomes clear: you need to escape from this village before it catches you...

FOLLOW TIM INTO THE VILLAGE STAY BACK IN THE CITY
