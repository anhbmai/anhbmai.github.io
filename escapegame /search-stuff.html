Night of the Arrival
11:45 PM - Tim left the tavern to sleep at Werner's estate right before a torrential rainstorm hit. Wolves were heard howling nearby.
Werner left behind a cryptic personal diary. However, it is written entirely in Romanian.
Eric blocked Marie from taking the diary, suggesting the priest at the village church look at it tomorrow morning.
The Following Morning
Tim's body was missing from Werner's cottage; only a deep bloodstain remained on the floor.
The body was later discovered at the base of a small river waterfall. All major veins and arteries were cleanly slashed open.
Marie Pularis (Town Gossip)
Claims Werner's throat was torn open by a werewolf six years ago in the forest during a full moon.
Terrified of the curse, she claims the beast returned to slaughter Tim and drain all his blood.
Dr. Ionescu (Physician)
Insists Werner wasn't killed by a wolf, but a jagged knife. Crucially, they couldn't stop Werner's wounds from bleeding.
Gregor Pularis (Mayor)
Claims Werner once killed a werewolf, cursing his entire bloodline with unhealing wounds.
